# RipCo coastal imagery

The lifestyle artwork is fictional and was generated with the built-in OpenAI
image-generation tool. It is not beach-camera footage, real model detection
output, or a photograph of Palm Beach. The app screen is a real capture supplied by the owner; displayed values are
historical screenshot content, not live conditions.

## Final website assets

- `beach-family.webp`: wide oblique aerial family scene, 1672 × 941.
- `beach-family-mobile.webp`: smaller 1000 × 562 version of the same scene.
- `beach-phone.webp`: matching close over-the-shoulder view, 1672 × 941.
- `beach-flags.webp`: illustrative red-and-yellow swimming flags, 1536 × 1024.
- `beach-camera.webp`: fictional coastal webcam and surf scene, 1672 × 941.
- The How it works interactive schematic is authored SVG, not camera footage or an actual model result. It illustrates a simplified sandbar rip. The risk-card crop uses the same unaltered owner-supplied app capture.
- `screens/home-app.webp`: actual owner-supplied 621 × 1264 app screenshot,
  encoded losslessly with metadata removed and composited using HTML/CSS.
- `og-coastal.png`: 1200 × 630 social preview, generated beach artwork with
  deterministic vector text composited over it.

white foam lines cutting diagonally from lower left toward upper centre, pale warm
sand fills right half. In the right third of the composition, at approximately 72

for overlaying white website headline. Natural luminous morning light, warm sand

photography, realistic natural ocean and correct human anatomy. No text, no
letters, no logo, no watermark. This is fictional lifestyle imagery, not a real
beach camera or rip-detection output.
