01 / A DIFFERENT VIEW OF THE WATER

The ocean doesn’t
show you everything.

A calm patch of water can hide a powerful rip. RipCo uses computer vision on existing public beach cameras to help you see what’s happening before you head in.

Likely rips are outlined on the camera view, with the model’s confidence alongside them. One more source of information. The flags and lifeguards always come first.

04 / WHY IT MATTERS

Familiar water.
Often unseen risks.

The need behind RipCo is real. These findings are why we’re building another way to understand the surf.

Coastal drowning deaths in Australia, 2024 to 2025
154
coastal drowning deaths in Australia in 2024–25, the highest number on record1
Share of beach drowning deaths involving a rip current
1 in 3
or more of beach drowning deaths involve a rip current, which makes rips Australia’s number one coastal hazard1
People who cannot spot a rip
3 in 4
people surveyed by Surf Life Saving Australia could not spot a rip from the beach2

MORE INFORMATION. THE SAME GOLDEN RULE.

The flags always come first.

RipCo is an aid, not a lifeguard. It can miss a rip. Swim between the red and yellow flags and follow the lifesavers and lifeguards on duty.
