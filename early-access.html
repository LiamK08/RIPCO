You’re on the list

You will get an email when RipCo goes live at Palm Beach. Until then the rules on the sand do not change: swim between the red and yellow flags and follow the lifesavers and lifeguards on duty.

Your data

What happens to your email

What will you do with my address?

Send you RipCo updates: the launch, and new beaches. I do not share or sell it, and every email has a way to leave the list. The full detail is on the privacy page.

Does the app track my location?

Location is optional. If you allow it, the app shows the beaches near you; without it, you can pick beaches from the map. Details on the privacy page.

Does the app watch people on the beach?

No. The model looks at the water, and the feeds are public streams that already exist. Details on the privacy page.
