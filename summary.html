RipCo: rip current detection on public beach cameras

A free iPhone app that reads the public camera already pointed at the beach and outlines likely rip currents on the live view. A Year 12 Design and Technology major work, Sydney, 2026.

www.ripcosafety.com/summary · RipCo, Year 12 Design and Technology major work, 2026

My own survey of beachgoers: 78% could not confidently identify a rip; 84% would use a mobile tool that shows rips on a live camera; 69% already check conditions before they go.

How it works

  1. A camera that already exists

    RipCo reads a public beach camera feed. Nothing is mounted or maintained.

  2. A model reads each frame

    It looks for a darker channel, a gap in the breaking waves and foam moving out to sea.

  3. An outline on the live view

    A likely rip is outlined in red with the model’s confidence. The flags line stays on screen.

What the app refuses to say

  • That a beach is safe. Each card shows a level of risk instead.
  • Whether to swim. The flags and the lifesavers decide that.
  • That it can see at night, in rain or in glare. It marks detection unavailable.
