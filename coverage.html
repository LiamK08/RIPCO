Extending coverage

What it takes to add a beach

Detection needs a public camera with a clear, steady view of the surf zone, a feed the app is allowed to read, and enough local footage to check the model against that beach. Conditions data is already available for any beach with coordinates, which is why the map has 26 beaches and detection has one.

Rips look different from beach to beach and camera to camera. A model that works at Manly is a starting point for the next beach, and each one has to be tested before the app shows an outline there.
