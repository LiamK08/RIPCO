Extending coverage

What it takes to add a beach

Detection needs a public camera with a clear, steady view of the surf zone, and enough local footage to check the model against that beach. Conditions data is available for any beach with coordinates, which is why this page tracks 26 beaches and detection has been tested at one.

Rips look different from beach to beach and camera to camera. A model that works at Manly is a starting point for the next beach, and each one has to be tested before the app shows an outline there.
