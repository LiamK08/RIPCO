Features

What’s in the app

Rip detection is the reason the app exists. Around it sit the conditions, a risk score, alerts, live cameras and two community tools, so you can check one beach in one place before you leave the house.

The home screen, annotated

What each part of the screen is for

The first screen you see when you open the app. It answers one question: what is the water doing at my beach? Redrawn from the app’s home screen.

1 2 3 4 5 6
  1. Weather now. Air temperature and sky at your location, first, so you can decide before you leave.
  2. Near you. How many beaches are within reach and how many have a camera streaming live, with one row of conditions across them.
  3. Patrol line. Patrol status where it is known, and “Patrol unknown” where it is not. The instruction is the same either way: check the flags.
  4. Live tag. The camera at this beach is streaming. Rip detection itself has only been tested at Palm Beach.
  5. Risk score. A number out of 100 built from the live conditions, with a label beside it; 55 reads Moderate risk in amber. How it is built.
  6. Detect. The live camera view with the rip outline, one tap from any screen. Map, Alerts and Profile sit beside it.
01 · The core feature

Live camera with rip detection

The live view from your beach camera, with likely rips outlined in red and a confidence figure beside each outline. I trained and tested the model on a public camera at Palm Beach; other beaches with a camera stream live without detection.

Tested at Palm BeachPrototype

Red outline
Where the model sees the signature of a rip in the current frame.
Confidence
How sure the model is, as a percentage, to weigh against what you can see.
Unavailable
At night, in heavy rain or strong glare the image is not usable, and the app says detection is unavailable.
Never
A “safe” verdict. A frame with no outline means no rip signature in that frame.

02 to 07

The tools around it

Each one describes the beach. The risk score summarises the conditions as a level of risk, and none of the tools gives a verdict on whether to swim.

02

Safety brief

A short plain-English summary of what the water is doing at your beach right now, with the reminder to swim between the flags built in.

03

Conditions and risk score

Water and air temperature, wind and swell for your beach, and a risk score out of 100 built from them, shown as a level of risk above the patrol line.

04

Alerts

The app watches the beaches you save and flags changing conditions before you head out.

05

Live cameras

Beaches with a public camera stream live and carry a Live tag. Detection runs on the stream only where I have tested it.

06

Shark Watch

Hazard sightings reported by other beachgoers, shared with everyone nearby. Reports are unverified; a lifeguard closure overrides the app.

07

Ask RipCo

A conversational assistant for conditions and beach-safety questions, built to point you to the flags rather than give a verdict.
