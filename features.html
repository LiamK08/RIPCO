How to read the risk score ↗

The day, at a glance. Air temperature and sky conditions at your location, right at the top of the home screen.

Your coastline, together. Nearby beaches, streaming cameras and a row of water, air, wind and swell readings in one place.

Check the flags first. The captured screen says “Patrol unknown, check the flags”. Confirm the patrol and follow advice on the beach.

A view of the water. The Live tag refers to a camera stream. It does not mean rip detection is running; detection has only been tested at Palm Beach.

A reading with context. The captured Manly Beach card shows 55, Moderate risk. This score is separate from detection confidence and is not permission to swim.

Your next view, one tap away. Home, Map, Detect, Alerts and Profile keep the main parts of the prototype within reach. Detect opens the camera view.

A little more
beach awareness.

Open a feature to explore its role in the prototype. These tools add context; none gives a verdict on whether to swim.

02Safety briefThe essentials, in plain English.Explore feature +

A short summary of conditions at your beach, with the reminder to swim between the flags kept close at hand.

Read the safety commitment ↗
03Conditions & riskMore context for your beach day.Explore feature +

Water and air temperature, wind and swell sit alongside a risk score. Read the score together with its label and the patrol information.

Understand the two readings ↗
04AlertsKeep your beaches close.Explore feature +

Saved beaches help you follow changing conditions before heading out. An alert is a reason to check the beach again, not a guarantee that every change will be detected.

Understand the limits ↗
05Live camerasSee the beach before you go.Explore feature +

Public camera streams offer another view of the water. Camera availability and tested rip detection are different things; Palm Beach is the only detection test location so far.

Explore the coastline ↗
06Shark WatchAwareness, shared by the community.Explore feature +

The prototype includes hazard reports from other beachgoers. Reports are unverified, and a lifeguard closure always overrides what the app shows.

Read the safety principles ↗
07Ask RipCoA conversation about conditions.Explore feature +

A conversational assistant for conditions and beach-safety questions. The design points you towards the flags and local patrol advice; it cannot decide whether swimming is safe.
